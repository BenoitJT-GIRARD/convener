Before you answer

This short, optional survey is only sent to people we have recorded as present at this event. Three questions, all short.

Your browser encrypts your answers before they are sent, using this event’s own key — the same key, and the same protection, as your registration. Nobody — including us — can read your answers until an organiser decrypts them as part of running the event.

Your answers are anonymous. Nothing here — no name, no address, no matching code — is sent alongside them, and that is deliberate. Because of that, we cannot find your own answers to show them to you, correct them or erase them individually once sent: there is nothing on file that says which answers are yours. Please do not write your name, your email address, or anything else that could identify you or anyone else, in the free-text box below.

All answers for this event, like your registration, are permanently destroyed together with the event’s key 90 days after the event. Questions about this survey can be sent to reading-group@example.test.
