Before you answer

This survey is sent only to people who attended.

Your answers are encrypted in your browser before they are sent, and only the organisers can read them.

Your answers are anonymous. Nothing that identifies you is sent with them, so we cannot show, correct or delete your answers once they are sent. Please leave out anything that could identify you or someone else.

All answers are permanently deleted 90 days after the event. For any question about this survey, contact us.
