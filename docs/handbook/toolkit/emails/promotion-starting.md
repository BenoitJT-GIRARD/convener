# Email — The promotion is starting

*To the speaker, three weeks before the talk, just before the series starts
announcing it. The announcement carries their name, their affiliation, their
title and their abstract; a speaker who learns of it from a notification has
been told last about their own talk.*

*Send it before the first post goes out, not after. Once the announcement is
published, a correction is a correction — and the whole point of this message
is to collect the corrections whilst they are still free.*

---

**Subject:** We are starting to announce your {{ instance.short_name }} talk on {{ speaker.date }}

Dear {{ speaker.first_name }},

We are about to start announcing your seminar, and we wanted you to hear it
from us first.

**This is what we are going to publish.** Please read it as your audience will
read it:

- **Speaker:** {{ speaker.name }}, {{ speaker.affiliation }}
- **Title:** {{ speaker.title }}
- **When:** {{ speaker.when }}
- **Abstract:**

> {{ speaker.abstract }}

If anything there is wrong, out of date or simply not how you would put it,
tell us and we will change it. A shorter abstract, a different title, an
affiliation you have since left — all of that is easy today and awkward once
the posts are live.

**Where it goes.** The announcement is posted on {{ instance.organisation }}, on
LinkedIn, and through the mailing lists and institute newsletters our
volunteers reach. The full list is the one the series keeps in the workspace,
and it grows; if there is somewhere you would rather we did not post, say so.

**Two things that help more than anything else.** Please register on
{{ instance.organisation }} ({{ instance.forum_host }}) and sign up to your own talk.
The series is built on a discussion before and after each seminar, and a
speaker who is not in the thread of their own seminar cannot answer anyone in
it. It takes a couple of minutes and it is the single thing that makes the
discussion work.

**Share it if you would like to.** Anything we post is yours to repost, and
your reminder nearer the time carries the forum thread. There is no obligation
at all.

Looking forward to it,

{{ host_1.name }}
*for {{ instance.organisation }} team*

---

## Notes for the volunteer sending this

- This goes out **before** the first announcement, not alongside it. The
  checklist puts it at T-3 weeks for that reason, ahead of the LinkedIn post
  and the forum announcement.
- The places the series announces in are configuration, not a fixed list. This
  message says what they are in general terms on purpose, so that adding a
  channel does not silently make an old e-mail untrue. If a speaker asks for
  the exact list, read it off the promotion lines of their checklist.
- If they send corrections, put them on the record before the posts go out.
  The announcement is built from the record, not from the reply sitting in
  somebody's inbox.
- Nothing here mentions the recording. Whether a recording is published is a
  question we ask afterwards, in its own message
  ([recording consent](consent-request.md)), and it is not settled by a
  sentence in a promotion e-mail.
