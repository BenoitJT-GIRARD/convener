# Email — The video is online

*To the speaker, once the recording of their talk is published.*

*This message can only be sent after the speaker has answered the
[recording consent](consent-request.md) question and the answer recorded is
that they agreed. Until then there is no published video to write about, and
a message announcing one would be announcing something nobody permitted. If
you have reached this line and the answer has not been recorded, the message
to send is the consent request, not this one.*

---

**Subject:** Your {{ instance.short_name }} talk is now online

Dear {{ speaker.first_name }},

Thank you again for giving us your permission to publish the recording of
*{{ speaker.title }}*. It is now online:

**{{ speaker.youtube_url }}**

The discussion that followed your talk is not in it — we stop the recording
before the discussion begins, so that people can speak freely — and neither is
anything else about you beyond what you agreed to.

The conversation continues on the forum thread of your seminar, where the
summary of the discussion has been posted:
{{ speaker.forum_thread }}

Please do share the video wherever you would like to. And if you ever change
your mind about it being public, write to us: we will record your answer and
the recording comes down. That stays true for as long as it is up, and you do
not owe us a reason.

Thank you again for speaking in the series.

Warm regards,

{{ host_1.name }}
*for {{ instance.organisation }} team*

---

## Notes for the volunteer sending this

- **Check the answer before you write.** The workspace shows the recorded
  answer on the speaker's page. If it is anything other than *they agreed* —
  including no answer at all — this message is not the one to send.
- The video address in this message is the one on the record. If the record
  has no address yet, fill it in first rather than pasting a link into the
  e-mail: the public feed links what the record holds, and an address that
  exists only in a sent e-mail is an address nobody can find again.
- A speaker who withdraws afterwards is an ordinary outcome, not a problem.
  Record the refusal the same day; that takes the recording out of the public
  feed on the next rebuild, and somebody still has to take the video down from
  YouTube by hand.
