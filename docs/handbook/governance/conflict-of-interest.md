# Conflicts of interest

We welcome talks that involve commercial or other interests — as long as these four conditions are met. The Board checks them when [validating a speaker](selection-criteria.md) and before [publishing a recording](../workflow/4-after.md).

1. **Science stays at the centre.** A talk is a scientific contribution, not a product pitch.
2. **Demonstrations are fair.** Results that are not peer-reviewed must be clearly flagged as such. How that flag is actually made in the session — who says it, when, and what happens when the speaker does not — is in the [run of show](../toolkit/run-of-show.md#results-that-have-not-been-peer-reviewed).
3. **Everything is disclosed.** Any interest — money, collaborations, close ties — is declared by the speaker, by whoever recommended them, and by the organising team. Commercial disclosures appear on the slides and in the video description.
4. **Publishing can be reconsidered.** Depending on the content, the Board may decide a talk is not put on YouTube.
