# Phase 4 — After the webinar

Fast and simple beats elaborate. Aim to close everything within a few days.

## Capture the discussion

During the discussion phase, enable Zoom's live transcription — **CC / Live Transcript → Enable Auto-Transcription** — and **Save Transcript** at the end (a `.txt` file). No video or audio recording of the discussion is needed. Turn it on right when the discussion begins; it only captures from that point.

## Post the discussion summary

Turn the transcript into a short, readable summary and post it on the forum, under the announcement thread — [template](../templates/forum-post-summary.md). The summary is a **synthesis, not a literal transcript**: give the necessary context, be diplomatic, and organise the discussion coherently.

## 🚪 Gate 2 — Publication

Before the recording goes public:

- A member of the Editorial Board validates publication (**lazy consensus** — approved unless vetoed within 72h). This is a compliance check: conflicts of interest, commercial content. Some talks may be decided not to publish.
- Once cleared, send the recording to the YouTube responsible for the TEATIME channel, and announce it on the forum.

## Thank the speaker

Send a short, warm thank-you — [template](../templates/emails/thank-you.md). Mention the engagement and any audience numbers; speakers value the reach. This small step greatly improves the experience for invited speakers.

## Record the metrics

Fill the four numbers in `data/events.yml` for this event:

- `registrations` · `live_peak` (peak live attendance) · `youtube_views_30d` · `forum_replies`

LinkedIn analytics can be exported as CSV and added to the Drive. These numbers feed the season review — see [Editorial Board](../governance/editorial-board.md).

## Close the loop

- [ ] **Update the runbook.** If something went wrong or could be smoother, fix the docs now — while it is fresh. This is the last item of the [post-event checklist](../checklists/post-event.md), and it is what keeps this handbook alive.
