# Phase 3 — Hosting day

The webinar runs on a **Thursday, 12:30–14:00 CET**.

## Timeline

| Time (CET) | What |
|---|---|
| 12:00 | Zoom room opens. Organizers / co-hosts can connect. |
| 12:15 | Co-hosts connect, check everything works, apply the [Zoom background](../assets/zoom-background.png). |
| 12:20 | Speaker joins (at least 10 minutes early). |
| 12:25 | Quick final check with the speaker. |
| 12:30 | Webinar starts. |

Run the **[day-of checklist](../checklists/day-of.md)** (DO-CONFIRM) about 60 seconds before going live.

## The two day-of roles

- **Host/MC** — introduces the speaker, keeps time, runs the Q&A, closes the session.
- **Tech-Producer** — admits attendees, watches the chat, shares links, keeps an eye on the recording.

A coherent series sends one voice: even though roles rotate, the audience should feel a consistent host.

## Run-of-show (~90 minutes)

| Time | Segment | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Welcome + housekeeping (recording notice, how to ask questions) | Host/MC |
| 0:02 | Introduce The Example Collective & the series concept | Co-host 2 |
| 0:04 | Introduce the speaker and their work | Host/MC (Co-host 1) |
| 0:06 | Talk | Speaker |
| ~0:50 | Q&A — moderated discussion | Host/MC |
| ~1:25 | Closing | Host/MC |

Use the [intro scripts](../templates/intro-scripts.md) for the two introductions.

## Q&A organisation

- **Sources & priority:** forum questions first (if not already answered), then live questions from the chat. Whenever possible, link a live question back to a forum thread.
- **Co-hosts alternate** asking questions.
- When relaying a question, **do not name the person who asked it**.
- Co-hosts read and relay questions — it keeps the discussion structured. Occasionally, a participant who raises their hand can be given the floor directly.
- Coordinate quietly on a private channel (e.g. WhatsApp) to avoid repeats; a shared list of questions and their source helps.

## Hosting principles

- **Start on time.**
- Online talks give speakers little feedback — make the discussion feel rich. **Never close with "No more questions? Okay, we finish."** Instead: *"We still have many interesting questions, but we're running out of time — the conversation can continue on The Example Collective."*
- Close by thanking the speaker and the audience and — if known — announcing the next speaker and topic, and that a new forum thread is open for questions.
