# Conflict-of-interest policy

We welcome seminars that involve commercial or other interests — provided the conditions below are met. This policy is applied at [Gate 1 — Selection](selection-criteria.md) and at [Gate 2 — Publication](../workflow/4-after.md).

## Conditions

1. **Scientific content stays central.** A talk is a scientific contribution, not a product pitch.
2. **Demonstrations are rigorous and unbiased.** Non-peer-reviewed results must be clearly stated as such.
3. **Full transparency.** Conflicts of interest — financial ties, collaborations, academic proximity, network affiliations — must be declared by the speaker, by whoever recommended them, and by the organizing team. Commercial disclosures should appear on the presentation slides and in the video description.
4. **Publication may be reconsidered after the live event.** Depending on the content, the Editorial Board may decide a given talk is not published on YouTube (🚪 Gate 2). Sponsorship models may be considered to support The Example Collective's costs.
