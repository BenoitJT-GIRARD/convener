# Intro scripts

The two spoken introductions at the start of the webinar. Keep them short and warm — together about four minutes.

## Script 1 — Introducing The Example Collective (Co-host 2)

*Derived from the [editorial line](../governance/editorial-line.md) — keep it consistent with it.*

> Welcome, everyone, and thank you for joining us. This is a seminar of **The Example Collective Monthly Reading Group** — a community series on behavioural science. Our idea is simple: a talk is the start of a conversation, not the end. There is a discussion thread on our forum before and after every talk, and the Q&A today is a real discussion. If you have questions, drop them in the chat or on the forum and we will relay them. Now, over to my co-host to introduce today's speaker.

## Script 2 — Introducing the speaker (Co-host 1 / Host)

*Fill from the bio collected with the [talk details email](emails/talk-details.md).*

> Today we are delighted to host **{{speaker}}**. {{bio_paragraph}} Today, {{speaker_first}} will present **{{title}}** — {{one_line_topic}}. {{speaker_first}}, thank you so much for accepting our invitation; it is a real pleasure to have you. The floor is yours — you can share your screen.

*Example from a past session: "Today we host Anonymous, also known as Niko. Niko is a London-based neuroscientist and Senior Research Software Engineer at the Neuroinformatics Unit, working across the Sainsbury Wellcome Centre and the Gatsby Computational Neuroscience Unit at UCL. He leads the development of Movement, a Python package for analysing animal motion… Niko, thank you for accepting our invitation — the floor is yours."*
