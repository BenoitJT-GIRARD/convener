# Roles & ownership

The series is run like an open-source project: anyone can contribute, a small body curates and validates, and everything is transparent. Roles are **functions, not people** — the model works the same for 4 volunteers or 30.

## The four tiers

| Tier | Role | Can do | Becomes one by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Contributor** | Whoever wants to help | Pick up an open task, propose a speaker, volunteer as co-host / Tech | Self-service: read [Start here](start-here/index.md) |
| **Event Owner** *(= MCP)* | DRI of **one** webinar | Owns one webinar's pipeline end to end, runs its runbook | Volunteering for a free slot — confirmed by the Editorial Board |
| **Editorial Board** | Curatorial & governance body | Carries the editorial line; votes speaker selection; validates publication; onboards Event Owners; adjusts policies | Co-optation by the Board, formalised at the annual renewal |
| **Architecte** *(you + 1-2)* | System architect | Designs and evolves the architecture: repo structure, data model, workflow design, the Chantier B layer; sets technical direction | Co-optation — small design core |

Promotion is **earned trust** — it is both the gatekeeping mechanism and the motivation ladder.

!!! warning "The Architecte is a design role, not a support role"
    The Architecte is *not* "the person to ping whenever something breaks". Operational help comes from the docs (self-service) and the Editorial Board — never from a single help desk. The Architecte *shapes* the system; they do not *repair* it on demand. It is a direction role, not a technician post.

## DRI — one owner, always

Every speaker entry and every webinar has exactly **one owner** — a Directly Responsible Individual. Never blank once active. The owner is accountable for the entry *moving* — not for doing every sub-task alone. This single rule is what stops things falling through the cracks.

## The two gates

| Gate | When | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 🚪 **Gate 1 — Selection** | A `lead` becomes an invited speaker | Two-thirds supermajority of the Editorial Board (default 4/6), ~2-week voting window |
| 🚪 **Gate 2 — Publication** | Content goes public (YouTube, forum summary) | Lazy consensus — one Board member approves, validated unless vetoed within 72h |

Everything else is **autonomy**: the Event Owner advances without asking permission.

## Day-of role contracts

For each webinar, two roles are assigned (they rotate):

- **Host/MC** — introduces the speaker, keeps time, runs the Q&A, closes the session.
- **Tech-Producer** — admits attendees, watches the chat, shares links, monitors the recording.

The other organizers are off or backup. See [Hosting day](workflow/3-hosting.md).
