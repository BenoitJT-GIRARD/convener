Propose a speaker

Bring us your talk.

Propose yourself, or someone whose work you admire — early-career researchers especially welcome. We ask who they are, what they would talk about, and how to reach them; a volunteer from the Editorial Board reads every proposal and gets back to you.

01 Before you start

What to expect

A proposal takes a few minutes: the speaker’s name and contact details, a preliminary title and short abstract, and who is proposing them if not themself. Nothing about a candidate speaker is published without their own, separate consent, later, if the talk is selected.

It is hosted on Tally, a third-party form service — the only part of this process that is. Questions about a proposal, or a request to withdraw one, go to reading-group@example.test.
