# Editorial line

What the series stands for.

## What this series is

A community-run series of online seminars centred on **behaviour** — its mechanisms, its measurement, and the tools and methods used to study it. It exists to make behavioural science **accessible, discussed, and connected across labs**, around a real conversation rather than a one-way lecture.

## Our values

- **Discussion-driven.** The talk is the start of the conversation, not the end. The forum thread before and the Q&A after are first-class — never an afterthought.
- **Early-career-friendly.** We deliberately make room for emerging researchers and less-visible voices. A great talk from a postdoc is worth as much as a famous name.
- **Open science.** We favour open tools, open data and reproducible methods, and we publish our recordings.
- **Behaviour-centred.** Whatever the technique — optogenetics, tracking software, modelling — the question that anchors a talk is a *behavioural* one.
- **Rigorous and honest.** Non-peer-reviewed results are welcome if labelled as such; commercial interests are welcome if fully disclosed. Science stays central.

## Our tone

Warm, curious and unpretentious. We thank speakers generously, we make the discussion feel rich, and we never let a speaker feel an online talk fell flat.

## What we will not do

- We are not a stage for product marketing — commercial content is welcome only when scientific content stays central and interests are disclosed.
- We do not chase big names for prestige at the expense of the early-career mission.
- We do not let the series become a one-way broadcast: if there was no discussion, we have not done our job.
