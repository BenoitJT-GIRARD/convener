# Email — Thank-you

*To the speaker, after the talk.*

*It says nothing about publishing the recording. Whether a recording goes
online is the speaker's answer to give, and it is asked for in its own
message ([recording consent](consent-request.md)) — so this one announces the
question rather than its answer.*

---

**Subject:** Thank you for your {{ instance.short_name }} talk!

Dear {{ speaker.first_name }},

Thank you so much for your talk — we, and the audience, really enjoyed it. There was strong engagement and a rich discussion, with around {{ speaker.live_peak }} people joining live.

We are summarising the discussion on the forum, and we will send you the link. We also recorded the session, as we do for every seminar; we will write to you separately to ask whether we may publish that recording, and it stays unpublished unless you tell us we may.

It was a real pleasure to host you. We hope to stay in touch, and the conversation can continue any time on {{ instance.organisation }}.

Warm regards,
{{ host_1.name }}
*for {{ instance.organisation }} team*

---

## Notes for the volunteer sending this

- The live peak comes off the record. Fill it in on the wrap-up before you
  send this, which is the order the checklist puts the two lines in; an
  unfilled number shows up in the message as a missing marker rather than as
  a plausible guess.
- Do not fold the consent question into this e-mail. It is asked properly,
  once, in [recording consent](consent-request.md) — a request that also
  explains what would be published and how to withdraw. Two asks produce two
  answers, and nobody can tell afterwards which one the speaker meant.
