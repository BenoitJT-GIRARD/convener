# D-17 — Typography: one free, self-hosted family in place of two licensed ones

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

The original identity specifies two commercially licensed typefaces. The
zero-cost constraint rules out a commercial licence on a public-facing
surface, so a substitute is not a stylistic preference — it is forced.

## Decision

A single free, self-hosted, variable typeface (Archivo, at two weights)
replaces both original families. Archivo is the standard open substitute for
the original body face.

For the display face, a stylistically closer alternative was available and
was set aside: the headings it would carry are long, academic talk titles,
and a stylised display face costs legibility exactly at the size it is
actually read.

**Self-hosting is not negotiable.** The showcase previously loaded fonts
from a third-party host, which sends every visitor's address to that third
party — on a site that also carries its own data-protection notice. A
self-hosted variable family is lighter than two separately hosted families
regardless.

## Rejected

A display face closer in character to the original heavy titling face, and
loading fonts from a third-party host.

## Cost

Some stylistic distance from the original display face's character, and one
family now carries a job two used to share between them.
