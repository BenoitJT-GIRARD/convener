# D-10 — The video channel belongs to the organisation

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

Recordings previously depended on a channel belonging to someone outside the
organisation: an external contact in the loop for every publication, and
recordings that a viewer looking for the organisation's own channel could
not find.

## Decision

Create an organisation channel on a brand account — ownership that transfers
with the organisation rather than a person — instead of continuing to depend
on a third party's channel.

## Rejected

Continuing to publish through a channel the organisation does not own.

## Cost

A new account to create and hand over as part of the organisation's shared
vault ([D-11](d-11-organisation-accounts-and-vault.md)) — no ongoing cost
beyond the setup already required by that decision.
