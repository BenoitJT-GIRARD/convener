Information and data

What we hold, and for how long.

Registering for a seminar, being recognised in the room, and receiving a certificate afterwards all involve a small amount of personal data. This page says what it is for and how to reach us about it; the full record it is drawn from is linked below, so this page never states a figure that record could later disagree with.

01 The record

Registration, attendance and certificates

What is collected, on what basis, who can reach it, for how long, and what protects it is set out in full in our data-protection record.

Read the data-protection record.

02 Your rights

Access, correction, erasure

You can ask to see, correct, withdraw consent for, or erase what is held about you, before the retention deadline the record above sets out. Write to reading-group@example.test for any of this.

Proposing a speaker is a separate process, with its own data — see Propose a speaker. The same address above reaches us about that too.
